Where we train

Set in Basildon, Essex, Basildon Sporting Village has become one of the leading leisure and sports centres in the area. It offers two swimming pools, which double up to provide one of the only places to train long course (50m racing) in Essex. Long course training is very much a privilege in the world of swimming, so we are fortunate to call this location our home.

Also on site we benefit from a fully equipped weights gym and fitness suite, an outdoor 400m running track, an indoor climbing wall, a sizeable sports hall for group land training, and free car parking.

BPSC swimmers train all of their sessions at Basildon Sporting Village — see the full squad timetables, or find out more about the venue on the Basildon Sporting Village website.
