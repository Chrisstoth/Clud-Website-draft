New Members Guide

Welcome to Basildon & Phoenix! Here's what to expect in your first few weeks:

Your Club Organiser invite arrives by email once your place is confirmed — this is how you'll manage membership, fees and details going forward. See the Club Organiser guide below for first-login steps.
Check Squad Timetables for your session days and times, and arrive 10 minutes early for your first session to meet the coach.
Kit needed: club-colour swimwear (available via our kit supplier — see Join Us), goggles, and a labelled kit bag. Full club kit isn't required on day one.
Monthly squad fees are collected by Direct Debit through Club Organiser — you'll set this up as part of registration.
Questions in the first few weeks usually go to your squad coach poolside, or the Membership team — see Contact Us.
Academy to Club Guide

Moving from our Academy (lessons) programme into a competitive club squad is a big step up — here's what changes:

Coaches recommend swimmers for a squad trial once they're ready; there's no need to ask, but let your Academy coach know if you're keen.
A successful trial moves your Club Organiser record from Academy to a squad membership — fees and training days update automatically from your next billing cycle.
Squad swimmers are expected to attend more regularly and start entering club-level galas — see the Gala Entry Guide below.
New kit requirements apply at squad level (club tracksuit, session kit) — the Membership team will confirm what's needed for your new squad.
Gala Entry Guide

How entering a gala works once your swimmer is racing for the club:

Open meets and their entry deadlines are listed on the Open Meets page — check the closing date, entries usually shut 2–3 weeks before the meet.
Entries themselves are submitted through Club Organiser (or a linked entry system for licensed meets) — your coach will confirm which events your swimmer is entering.
Entry fees are charged through Club Organiser once entries close — no separate payment needed.
On the day: arrive for the warm-up time on the meet's information sheet, not the first race time. Session and heat sheets are usually published a few days beforehand.
Club Organiser Guide

A quick walkthrough of the system parents use for membership admin:

First login: use the invite link emailed to you when you joined to set your password, then log in any time at cluborganiser.co.uk.
Update details: keep contact numbers, emergency contacts and medical/allergy information current under your swimmer's profile — coaches rely on this being accurate.
Fees & payments: check your Direct Debit status, view past payments and see any arrears from the Payments section.
Gala entries: confirm or withdraw from galas your swimmer's been entered into, and see entry fees as they're charged.
Trouble logging in or a system question? contact our Club Organiser Coordinator — see Club Committee for details.
