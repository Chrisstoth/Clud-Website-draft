Your information

Privacy & Cookies

What this website collects, why, who sees it, and what it remembers in your browser. In short: the site sets no cookies, doesn't track you, and only collects personal details when you send us a Join Us enquiry.

Who we are

This website is run by Basildon & Phoenix Swimming Club (BPSC), Basildon Sporting Village, Cranes Farm Road, Basildon, Essex SS14 3GR. The club is responsible for the personal information described on this page. For any question about it, email info@phoenixbasildonsc.org.

This page covers the website only. Members' records (membership, fees and Swim England registration) are kept in Club Organiser and covered by the club's own data protection arrangements. Club Organiser has its own privacy policy.

Join Us enquiries

When you send an enquiry from the Join Us page we collect:

  • your name and email address (for Masters enquiries, just your name and email)
  • the swimmer's name and date of birth
  • their swim lesson stage, or their Swim England number and current level
  • anything you add in the "Anything else we should know?" box

Why: to reply to you and arrange a trial or a place, because you've asked us to. Details about a child are given to us by their parent or guardian.

Who sees it: the enquiry is emailed to the club's membership inbox, and a copy is kept in the website's secure database so it isn't lost if the email goes astray. Only the Membership Team and the club's Webmaster can see these copies. We don't sell your details, share them with anyone else or use them for marketing.

How long we keep it: the copy is deleted from the website once the enquiry has been dealt with. If the swimmer joins the club, their details move into our membership records.

Spam protection: to stop the form being misused, the website remembers a scrambled version of the sender's internet (IP) address for one hour.

The members' area

Committee members, coaches and volunteers who update this website sign in to its admin area with their email address. We keep their name, email address and role so the right people can change the right things.

Club news and photos

Our news posts sometimes include swimmers' names and photographs, for example gala results and club events. These follow the club's Photography Protocol and Social Media Protocol. If you'd like a name or photo taken down, email info@phoenixbasildonsc.org and we'll remove it.

Cookies and what your browser remembers

This website doesn't set any cookies, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. That's why there's no cookie banner.

To save you repeating yourself, the site keeps a few small notes in your browser's own storage. They stay on your device and aren't sent to us or anyone else:

  • whether you chose light or dark mode
  • the squad you last looked at on Squad Timetables
  • the squads and club news you follow, so matching stories are marked "For you"
  • your choice of homepage view on race days
  • a copy of our partners list, seasonal look and Member Guides, so pages appear faster
  • for people who use the members' area: that you're signed in, and the time of your last activity, so you're signed out after an hour without activity

You can wipe all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings.

The map on our Venue page comes from Google Maps, and Google may set its own cookies when it loads. So the map only appears if you press Show map. Until then, nothing is loaded from Google Maps.

Services that help run the site

Like every website, ours relies on a few outside services. When you visit, they receive your device's internet address, because that's how the page reaches you:

  • IONOS hosts the website and the club's email. Like all web hosts, it keeps short-term records of visits to keep the site running and secure.
  • Supabase runs the website's database, which holds the news, galas and timetables you see, the members' area accounts and copies of Join Us enquiries.
  • Google Fonts provides the lettering the site uses.
  • jsDelivr delivers a piece of code the site needs to talk to its database.

Each of these companies only handles the information to provide its service to us. Links to other websites, such as Club Organiser, Facebook, Instagram, live results and live streams, take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you or your child, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email info@phoenixbasildonsc.org and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Last updated 18 November 2026. If we change how the website uses information, we'll update this page.
