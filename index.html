Join Us

There are two routes into the club. Pick the one that fits your swimmer and our membership team will get back to you about next steps.

Academy enquiry — swim lesson stage

Stage 6 is the realistic minimum for the Academy — and the older the swimmer, the higher the stage we'd expect them to be at. The Academy takes swimmers up to 12 years old; older swimmers are trialled directly for our other squads instead, so do still get in touch.
